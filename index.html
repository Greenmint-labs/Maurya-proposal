The Problem

A bad pour wastes
an entire heat
of 1,200 kg

With four furnaces each processing 1,200 kg per heat, the margin for error is extremely narrow. A wrong chemical composition or out-of-range pouring temperature is not caught until after the metal is in the mould.

🌡
No real-time composition alerts — spectro results are checked, but deviations are not flagged until after the pour proceeds
🏖
Sand property inconsistency — readings taken manually and inconsistently, no pattern tracking across shifts
📊
No batch traceability — when QA rejects a casting, there is no structured way to trace it back to the foundry conditions that produced it
